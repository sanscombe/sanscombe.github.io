Sylvy Anscombe.

Manuscript, 2026 (version of 4th November 2026).

A new, self-contained account of Kuhlmann's theory of tame valued fields, with shorter proofs of the Generalized Stability Theorem and of Henselian Rationality.

**Contents.**
1. Selected ingredients, notation, and conventions
2. Generalized Stability
3. Henselian rationality
4. Separably tame valued fields: the embedding theorem and Ax&ndash;Kochen/Ershov principles

Appendices: Frobenius-closed bases of function fields; the Fundamental Equality; more about strong bases.
